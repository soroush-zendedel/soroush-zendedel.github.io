Soroush Piri Zendedel

Known as Soroush Zendedel · Software Developer · Technical Artist · Writer

I am Soroush Piri Zendedel, known as Soroush Zendedel. I am a software developer, technical artist, and writer. I walk between thought and emotion, learning from technology and art, and try to move forward with more awareness and honesty. In my projects, I like to bring programming and technology together with art and the things I care about—such as music, design, and writing—and shape them into expressive digital experiences.

Professional experience

  • Freelance Software Developer · 2016–present: web and full-stack software development, with a focus on Python, Django, web applications, and Linux.
  • Computer Teacher and Computer Lab Coordinator · Mansour Educational Complex · for part of 2025.
  • Software Developer · Andishe and Ta'avon Zarin Zaris NGO · 2019–2022.

Education

  • Mansour Educational Complex: attended for middle school and high school.
  • University of Zabol: Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, 2012–2016.
  • Sistan and Baluchestan University: Computer Science studies, 2012–2014; later transferred to the University of Zabol.

Family and cultural roots

My father, Alireza Piri Zendedel, is a poet and an educator who taught in Iran’s public education system and was selected as a national exemplary teacher. He has written several books on culture, politics, and poetry, many of them focused on Sistan and the city of Zabol. His books include Delbaram Hamoonzad and Dar Chenbari az Sarab (In a Ring of Mirage). My connection to Sistan and Zabol, and my interest in writing and art, are also part of this family and cultural background.
